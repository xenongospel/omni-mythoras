## Second pass — outcome

### A. The LEAN

**Trace the DD1 plate — its finish and density, not its information
architecture.** ADR-0002 stays **Proposed**; the LEAN is recorded inside it under
*Lean for this pass*, along with its cost and the location a Turn Program would
occupy if it were built.

What decided it: MoP's badges carry roughly three numbers per unit, while a
Mythoras Field creature carries HP, Load, four Attributes, Speed, tags, four
keyworded Skill Cards, two trinkets and ailments — and POSTURE §0 forbids
dropping information to make an arrangement work. Reinforced by the fact that
only DD1 has ever been measured, so the other options had no tokens to apply.

**Correction made to the MoP evidence.** The claim that "MoP has no console" was
wrong. MoP has chrome on all four edges, including a Battle log dock and a bottom
strip carrying reserves, turn order and end turn. It has no *frame*. The axis is
framed plate vs frameless chrome on void. Fixed in ADR-0002 and
`reference/README.md`.

### B. Measured tokens applied

Palette and type scale are on the plate, replacing a warm-brown palette that
contained none of the measured values.

- Type is **proportional, not fixed px**: `--sp: calc(var(--plate-h) / 362)` is
  one reference pixel at 1080p, so §5.1's numbers are written literally and still
  scale. Verified at 1080p — name 47.97, class 19.99, HP 21.99, stat 18.99 on a
  17.98 pitch, plate 33.50% of window height.
- **No accent hue.** Gold `#c4a66a` is gone; it was never in the reference. State
  is carried by brightness, and red is HP-only.
- Two faces, one large step. Sizes with no measured row sit at body size.

Two bugs found and fixed while verifying: the corner bookend was covering the
map/inventory tab column, and dropping the `max-height` cap let the map SVG's
intrinsic height push the plate to 80% of the window.
